Wavelens privacy policy

English | 繁體中文

Effective 1 November 2026.

Wavelens collects no personal data, and sends nothing anywhere.

  • No internet access. The app does not ask for the internet permission, so it has no way to send anything off the phone: no analytics, no crash reports, no ads, no third-party services.
  • Microphone. Used only while you record, from the moment you tap Record until you tap Stop. While it runs, a notification says so. The take stays on the phone until you save or share it.
  • Files. The app reads only the files you pick in the system's file picker, and writes only where you choose in Save as.
  • Work in progress. The open clip, its undo steps and its markers are kept in the app's own storage on the phone, so that closing the app or the phone ending it in the background loses nothing. Other apps cannot read that storage, and uninstalling Wavelens deletes it.
  • Sharing. Only when you tap Share, and only to the app you choose there.
  • Notifications. Asked for only to show that a recording is running.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
